## Scene descriptions (expanded, for director/editor reference)

**Beat 1 — Hook (0:00–0:03)**
Camera: Slow drone descent, wide establishing shot. Shot pulls down from upper mountain ridge to reveal flat open grassland in the foreground — the empty plot. No movement from any human subject. Sound design: low wind, distant bird call, complete silence otherwise.
Asset: Real drone photo (836959163_*.jpg) used as motion base or reference angle for AI-extended establishing shot. No label required (real photo). If AI motion is added to extend the static photo, label "ภาพประกอบ / Illustration" must appear on those extended frames.

**Beat 2 — Owner enters (0:03–0:12)**
Camera: Low-angle push beginning at ground level (boots entering frame), rising to mid-shot as owner walks toward center of plot. Camera begins slow orbital arc. Owner carries blueprint roll under one arm, other arm free. He surveys the empty site ahead.
Asset: AI-generated character based on character-owner.md (dark navy long-sleeve shirt, round black/gold glasses, Apple Watch Ultra). Label "ภาพประกอบ / Illustration" in upper-left corner as a static HUD element — must not scroll or fade during orbital movement.

**Beat 3 — Blueprint unfurl (0:12–0:18)**
Camera: Orbital sweep continues, camera low. Owner faces the plot. Both hands raise the blueprint roll and unroll it slowly outward. The blueprint surface glows a faint electric blue at the edges — subtle sci-fi energy, not cartoon. Holds for 2–3 seconds on the open blueprint before the first pull.
Asset: AI-generated. Label persists.

**Beat 4 — First pull: bamboo scaffolding (0:18–0:26)**
Camera (AI frames): Close-up on owner's hands reaching into blueprint, pulling out glowing bamboo poles. Wide to show him tossing them onto the site in a sweeping arm gesture.
Camera (real photo cut): Immediate hard cut to drone photo of the multi-unit building — bamboo scaffolding covering all faces, white plastered exterior, bright overcast sky, mountain backdrop. Camera angle suggested: slow push in from the wide drone angle.
Asset (pull): AI-generated. Label "ภาพประกอบ / Illustration" on every AI frame.
Asset (building photo): Real — 836959163_18644608891048359_5489349789714552875_n.jpg. No label required. Short text overlay "ไม้ไผ่นั่งร้าน" in clean white sans-serif, lower left, 1.5 sec max.

**Beat 5 — Second pull: steel sheeting + concrete blocks (0:26–0:34)**

**Beat 6 — Building rises: orbital climax (0:34–0:42)**
Camera: Slow dramatic orbital drone sweep around the full multi-unit building. Camera stays low — below mid-building height — and completes a 120–180° arc. Mountains fill the background throughout. No cut; single sustained movement.

**Beat 7 — Final pull + speed escalation (0:42–0:48)**
