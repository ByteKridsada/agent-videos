## Idea 1 — Grand Orbit

**Format:** Single slow-burn cinematic reveal with full orbital camera payoff
**Length:** 50–60 seconds
**Best platform:** Facebook (primary); recut to 30 s for TikTok

### Hook options (first 3 seconds — Thai)

1. [Silence + wind sound] Wide drone shot of empty grassy plot, mountains behind. Title card fades in: "แค่ที่ดินเปล่า..."
2. (Voiceover, calm) "ทุกบ้านที่คุณเห็น... เริ่มจากกระดาษแผ่นเดียว"
3. (Low-angle push on feet walking across empty ground) Owner steps into frame; blueprint roll under his arm — no words, music swells.

### 3-Line Outline

**Act 1 — Empty world.** Drone descends from the mountain skyline to an open foreground plot (real: verified mountain backdrop in all 4 drone JPGs, F6). Owner walks in alone, rolls the blueprint open dramatically. Camera sweeps low around him.

**Act 2 — The pulls.** He reaches into the blueprint and pulls each material out in order — bamboo scaffolding poles, steel roofing sheets, concrete blocks, blue waterproofing tarp, large water tank (all verified on-site in F9). Each pull cuts to a real drone photo of the multi-unit building at the corresponding construction stage (F7: mid-plastered exterior, scaffolding; Screenshot 22.08.07: street-level near-complete). Camera orbits the building as each stage completes.

**Act 3 — The reveal.** Final orbital sweep at full speed. Building "snaps" complete. Cut to CGI render (2 sec, labeled "ภาพประกอบ / Illustration" at all times). Cross-fade back to real near-complete building drone shot. Owner stands in foreground, thumbs-up (real photo: Screenshot 22.06.44). ## Idea 2 — Northern Promise

**Format:** Emotion-first personal monologue over cinematic blueprint-pull
**Length:** 45 seconds
**Best platform:** Facebook

2. (Owner walks toward camera across empty plot; direct eye contact) No words for 3 sec, then: "กระดาษแผ่นนี้คือคำสัญญา"
3. (Close on blueprint being unrolled, hands in frame) "ทุกอย่างอยู่ในนี้แล้ว — ขอแค่ให้โอกาสผมดึงมันออกมา"

**Act 2 — The promise in action.** He unrolls the blueprint, reaches in, and pulls materials one by one. Each pull cuts to the townhouse row at a different stage — Stage 1: concrete columns and slab (755870425_*.jpg); Stage 2: walls up, bamboo scaffolding (835721679_*.jpg); Stage 3: street elevation, steel roof frame installed (755870426_*.jpg). **Act 3 — Close on trust, not on spectacle.** Final pull produces a miniature house. Owner sets it down gently; it becomes the building on-site (real: near-complete building drone). No CGI render in this idea — the payoff is the real site. Voiceover: "อยากเห็นผลงานจริง? ทักมาเลย" Owner looks at camera. Real thumbs-up photo (22.06.44) as outro still frame.

## Idea 4 — Every Material Has a Name

**Format:** Educational trust-builder — blueprint pulls as a material-naming walkthrough
**Length:** 45–60 seconds
**Best platform:** Facebook (primary), TikTok

### Hook options (first 3 seconds — Thai)

1. (Owner, direct to camera) "รู้มั้ยว่าบ้านหนึ่งหลัง ต้องใช้อะไรบ้าง? ผมดึงให้ดูเลย"
2. (Close on blueprint, hands in frame) "ช่างที่ดีต้องรู้วัสดุทุกชิ้น — และผมจะไม่ซ่อนอะไรไว้"
3. (Low angle, owner holds blueprint toward camera) "ก่อนจะมีบ้าน ต้องมีสิ่งเหล่านี้ก่อน"

### 3-Line Outline

**Act 1 — Setup.** Owner (AI character, labeled) stands on an empty plot holding the blueprint. Voice mode: senior builder. Camera frames him mid-shot; he addresses the viewer directly: "ทุกบ้านที่ผมสร้าง วัสดุทุกชิ้นมาจากแบบนี้"

**Act 2 — Named pulls.** He pulls each item from the blueprint; each pull is matched to a real on-site footage cut. Every item gets a Thai text label on-screen:
- ไม้แบบ (timber formwork) → real: lumber debris on ground, townhouse site
- ไม้ไผ่นั่งร้าน (bamboo scaffolding) → real: bamboo poles, townhouse row (835721679)
- แผ่นเหล็กหลังคา (metal roofing sheets) → real: steel frame, townhouse (755870425)
- อิฐมอญ / บล็อกคอนกรีต (concrete blocks) → real: visible block walls, townhouse
- แผ่นกันซึม (waterproofing sheet) → real: blue tarps on site
Voiceover for each item: one sentence in plain Thai on why it matters, no specs or prices.

**Act 3 — Payoff.** Final pull: the finished house. Owner sets it down, camera pulls back to reveal the real building behind him. CTA: "อยากรู้ว่าบ้านของคุณต้องใช้อะไร? ทักมาถามได้เลย — ฟรี ไม่มีข้อผูกมัด"

| Text overlays (material names) | Motion graphics | N/A |
| Townhouse near-complete for payoff | Real: 835721679 or 836959163 | None |

**Hero building: Townhouse row.** Reason: the specific materials (bamboo poles, timber, block walls, metal roof frame, blue tarps) are most visually distinct in the townhouse drone photos. The material-level detail is clearer from those angles.

### Risks / facts to verify first

- Voiceover about individual materials must remain generic ("ไม้ไผ่ช่วยค้ำยันระหว่างก่อสร้าง") — do not state brand names, grades, or standards not in the raw data.

## Idea 6 — Pull by Pull

**Format:** High-energy fast-cut TikTok montage, cut on beat
**Length:** 30 seconds
**Best platform:** TikTok (primary)

### Hook options (first 3 seconds — Thai)

1. [No voiceover — sound effect: blueprint unfurls with a cinematic whoosh] Owner holds blueprint, looks at camera, then turns to the plot.
2. (Fast title card, white on black) "สร้างบ้าน... ดึงออกมาจากแบบ" — cut to first pull on beat.
3. (Owner, direct and quick) "ไม่ใช่เวทมนตร์ — แต่มันดูเหมือน" — then music drops.

### 3-Line Outline

**Act 1 — The drop (0–8 sec).** Music beat drops. Owner reaches into blueprint; pulls out bamboo pole — cut (0.5 sec) to real bamboo on site. Pulls steel sheet — cut to real metal roof frame. Pulls concrete block — cut to real block walls. Each cut is exactly on beat.

**Act 2 — The build (8–22 sec).** Pulls increase in pace. Low-angle push toward multi-unit building as scaffolding appears. Quick drone orbit. Cut to ground-level view of near-complete facade (Screenshot 22.08.07). Each cut is faster than the last — 4 cuts in 2 seconds by the end of this act.

**Act 3 — The land (22–30 sec).** Owner tosses a miniature house from the blueprint; it lands and the building appears behind him in a single dramatic cut. Text: "โฮมมิ่ง คอนสตรัคชั่น — ทักเลย" + Messenger/LINE CTA. AI-disclosure label appears.

### Assets needed

| Asset | Source | Label required |
|---|---|---|
| Owner pull sequences (multiple) | AI-generated | "ภาพประกอบ / Illustration" — must survive fast cuts; consider static watermark |
| All 4 drone JPGs for material cuts | Real | None |
| Screenshot 22.08.07 for facade cut | Real | None |
| AI landing / building-appears effect | AI-generated | "AI simulation" label at minimum on that shot |

**Hero building: Multi-unit.** The scaffolding-to-white-plastered-facade arc is more visually dramatic at speed and reads as a single strong reveal.

### Risks / facts to verify first

- Label compliance is hardest on this format. When cuts are 0.5 sec, the "ภาพประกอบ" label must either be a persistent watermark or must flash clearly enough to be readable. Platform AI-disclosure requirements (TikTok) apply.
- Fast-cut energy may perform well on TikTok but may hurt Facebook watch time. If the primary goal is Facebook leads, this idea underserves that platform.
- Owner likeness [UNVERIFIED] — same as all other ideas.
