## Music Arc Note

The music opens at silence for the first three seconds — only wind and ambient site sounds during the hook drone descent. At 0:03, as the owner's boots enter frame, the symphonic score enters quietly: low strings and a single sustained brass note, establishing scale and gravitas. Through beats 2–5 (owner entry, blueprint unfurl, first and second pulls), the score builds in stages — each real-photo hard cut lands on a rhythmic hit or orchestral accent that rewards the pull-to-reveal mechanic. The orbital climax in beat 6 (Shot 11) drives the music to its widest, most sweeping moment: full orchestra, strings rising, brass swelling against the mountain backdrop. After the final pull in beat 7, the score accelerates with the camera speed-ramp, reaching full apex at the CGI flash cut (0:48) — a brief silence or single piano/bell hit immediately before the dusk reveal lands the scene as the emotional peak. For beat 9 (CTA), the score drops to a warm resolved coda — the same motif from the opening, but settled and confident, holding under the brand title until end of clip.

## Elements to Lock for Scene-Producer

### Character — เจ้าของ (Business Owner)
All AI shots (2–12) must use the same character generation. Lock these parameters across all generations before producing any individual shot:
- Build: stocky/chubby, medium height, Thai male, early-to-late 30s
- Face: round, full cheeks, small-to-medium eyes, warm smile, light mustache (optional per shot)
- Hair: short black, styled back (pompadour/side-swept), neat
- Glasses: round frames, black or black-gold metal, medium-large size — present in every shot except on-site variant; DO NOT remove
- Outfit: dark navy long-sleeve button shirt, dark jeans, black sneakers — consistent across all shots; DO NOT introduce any variation
- Accessories: Apple Watch Ultra on LEFT wrist (must be visible in close-up shots 6 and 7), rings on both hands
- Posture: upright, confident, not stiff

### Blueprint Prop — Consistency Lock
- Physical form: cylindrical scroll, 60–70 cm long, brown/cream exterior paper with weathered edges
- Glow behavior: edges light electric-blue when unrolled; interior surface activates with architectural line geometry in blue; portal depth visible when hand enters
- Energy color: electric blue ONLY — no variation to other colors across any shot
- FX quality: cinematic, grounded, subtle particle drift; NOT neon, NOT cartoon, NOT video-game HUD aesthetic

### Location — Construction Site
- Ground: dry compacted earth/grassland, northern Thailand character
- Background: mountain range visible in upper 30% of frame in wide shots; mountains must remain consistent across shots 2–12
- Sky: overcast-to-partly-cloudy — muted, cinematic; avoid deep blue tourist-sky
- No other people visible in AI shots

### Color and Light (AI shots 2–12)
- Color grade target: muted cinematic tones; warm earth on ground, cool grey-blue on mountains, neutral-to-warm on the character
- Avoid: over-saturated blues, HDR pop, Instagram-filter warmth
- Blueprint glow is the only saturated color element — it should pop against the muted scene palette

### On-screen Label — "ภาพประกอบ / Illustration"
- Position: upper-left corner, anchored to frame coordinates
- Style: white text (Prompt or Noto Sans Thai, 24–28pt equivalent at 1080×1920), semi-transparent dark pill background (50–60% opacity black)
- Layer: compositor HUD — topmost layer, must not be occluded by any other element
- Behavior: static (does not animate, scale, fade, or scroll with camera movement)
- Disappears ONLY on shots confirmed as pure real-photo (Shots 1 [if no AI motion], 8, 10, 11, 13, 15)
- Persists through all dissolve and transition frames involving AI or CGI content
