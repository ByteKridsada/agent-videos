## Story angles the material supports

- **Blueprint-to-built transformation anchor**: The drone photos show the real multi-unit building at multiple stages (structural frame, block walls, plastering, near-complete exterior). Paired with the CGI render of the finished building, they form a genuine before-during-after arc that can anchor the "house builds itself" concept without fabrication.
- **Scale and credibility**: The drone perspectives give natural cinematic sweep angles — wide establishing shots looking across open ground toward the mountainous northern Thailand skyline. These support the "awe-inspiring orbit" camera direction requested.
- **Materials on the ground as props**: Bamboo poles, timber planks, metal roofing sheets, blue tarps, bricks, and water tanks are all visibly present on-site. These real materials can serve as the specific items the owner "pulls from the blueprint" in the concept — grounding the sci-fi moment in genuine construction vocabulary.
- **Owner as protagonist**: Seven distinct photos establish the real-world reference for the character. The site photo (excavation, pointing) most directly maps to the opening scene of the concept. The thumbs-up photo (arched interior) maps to the final pride/reveal beat.
- **Early-stage plot as entry point**: The townhouse row at foundation/column stage shows an essentially bare structural skeleton — the closest raw material to the "empty plot" the concept requires. A low-angle push from that empty-looking foreground toward the building under construction is supported by existing aerial vantage points.
- **Render as "finished state" reference**: The CGI image confirms the intended completed look of the multi-unit building. It can be used as the revealed final frame of the clip, provided it is labeled "ภาพประกอบ / Illustration" per dos-donts.md.

## Cautions (vs dos-donts.md)

- **Fake-real imagery rule applies to the CGI render.** Screenshot 22.07.49 shows a polished 3D building render that could easily be mistaken for a real completed building. If used in the clip, it must carry a visible "ภาพประกอบ / Illustration" or "AI / CGI simulation" label at all times it appears on screen.
- **All AI-generated character imagery must be labeled.** The character-owner.md profile and the real owner photos are reference material only. Any AI-generated image of the owner character used in the clip (Midjourney, Flux, etc.) must carry "ภาพประกอบ / Illustration" per character-owner.md and dos-donts.md. The concept's "realistic as possible" requirement creates tension with this rule — the scriptwriter must plan label placement so it does not disappear during cinematic transitions.
- **Identifiable faces of bystanders.** Screenshot 22.03.21 contains at least two people other than the owner. Do not use this photo without blurring non-consenting faces.
- **No price, superlative, or guarantee language.** The raw data contains no pricing. The script must not introduce any price claim, "ถูกที่สุด", "ดีที่สุด", or construction timeline guarantee. Any construction timeline stated would be [UNVERIFIED].
- **The clip concept is metaphorical / fantastical.** The "pulls materials from a blueprint" sequence does not depict real construction process. This is acceptable as storytelling but the final clip should not imply that actual construction happens this quickly or magically — the CTA should ground viewers in reality (e.g., "ทักมาเลยเพื่อดูผลงานจริง").
- **Platform AI-disclosure label required.** TikTok and Facebook both require AI-content disclosure when AI visuals are published. This applies to any AI-generated frames in the final clip.
