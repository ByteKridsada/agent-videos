# Do / Don't and safety rules (active context)

> Criteria fact-reviewer uses for PASS/FAIL. Not legal advice: have a qualified person check advertising, real-estate and lending rules before treating them as final.
> Thai example phrases are matched against Thai scripts; keep them.

## Don't (say or show)
| Group | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Loan outcome promises | "ผ่านแน่นอน", "ผ่านทุกเคส" | Approval belongs to the lender |
| Superlatives | "ที่สุด", "อันดับ 1", "ดีที่สุด" | Unprovable |
| Price | "ถูกที่สุด", "ไม่มีค่าใช้จ่ายเพิ่มทั้งสิ้น" | Any exception becomes a dispute |
| Returns | "กำไรแน่นอน", "ราคาขึ้นแน่" | No one can guarantee |
| Warranty | Stating a warranty without scope | Must say what it covers, per contract |
| Fake urgency | "ของหมดแล้ว" when untrue | Destroys trust |
| Real people | Faces/voices/names of customers without permission | Privacy |
| Fake-real imagery | AI visuals that look like the company's real work | Destroys credibility |

## Do
- Every fact traces to `00_raw/` or `brand.md`.
- Offers with conditions: state the main conditions or point to where to see them.
- Label AI visuals that could be mistaken for real: illustration/simulation.
- Apply the platform's AI-disclosure label.
- Blur identifiable data (faces, license plates, house numbers) without permission.
