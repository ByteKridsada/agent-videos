# Audience Profile (active context)

## Primary audience
- Who (age, job, situation): Thai adults aged 28–45, working professionals or small business owners in Chiang Mai or Phayao, planning to build or buy their first family home. Often have a plot of land or are evaluating ready-built options in housing developments like Urban Town.
- What decision they are making: Whether to hire a construction company (and which one), or whether to buy a ready-built home vs. build custom — and how much it will cost.
- Platforms they actually use: Facebook (primary, for research and Messenger inquiries), TikTok (for inspiration and quick tours), LINE (for direct communication).

## Worries / fears
1. Getting scammed or overcharged — fear of contractors who take deposits and disappear or cut corners on materials.
2. The finished house not looking like what was promised — gap between renders/promises and reality.
3. Going over budget or timeline — unexpected costs, hidden fees, or long delays that disrupt family plans.

## Frequent questions
1. ราคาสร้างบ้านเริ่มต้นที่เท่าไหร่? (What's the starting price to build a house?)
2. ใช้เวลาสร้างนานแค่ไหน และมีตัวอย่างผลงานจริงให้ดูไหม? (How long does construction take, and can I see real completed projects?)

## What builds their trust
- Real photos and video of completed homes (not renders)
- Transparent pricing breakdowns or package details
- Customer testimonials from local buyers in Chiang Mai / Phayao
- Showing construction progress — demonstrates professionalism and follow-through
- Company registration, license, or warranty information

## What makes them leave
- Only promotional graphics with no real project proof
- Vague pricing ("contact for price" with no range)
- Stock photos or AI-generated house images not labeled as illustrations
- No response mechanism (no Messenger link, phone, or LINE)
